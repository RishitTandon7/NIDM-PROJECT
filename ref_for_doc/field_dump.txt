Field Visit Report (Submitted: 31-12-2025)
Project: NIDM-Funded GLOF Study
Area: East & North Sikkim (Tista River Basin)
Survey Dates: 19–28 December 2025
Coordinated by: Dr.Snehasish Ghosh, CO-PI-IUINDRR-NIDM “GLOFFIXEDWING” project
21 December 2025 – East Sikkim - Visited Tsomgo (Changu) Lake, Sarathang lakeNathula Pass, and surrounding high-altitude glacial valley areas. Field observations focused on lake condition, snow cover, geomorphology, and seasonal hydrological characteristics of the Tsomgo–Nathula sector.
22 December 2025 – Gangtok - Visited government and administrative offices for permit-related and official coordination. Discussions were held regarding access permissions, border-controlled areas, and future drone-based field surveys in East and North Sikkim.
23 December 2025 – East Sikkim - Covered Zuluk, Nathang Valley, Elephant Lake (Kupup Lake), Lampokhri Lake and Rongli region. Observations included lake distribution, alpine valley geomorphology, and hydrological linkages within the Silk Route sector of East Sikkim.
24 December 2025 – North Sikkim - Visited Tista River valley, Chungthang, and nearby areas affected by the 2023 GLOF event. Significant riverbank erosion, infrastructure damage, and valley-scale geomorphic changes were observed, highlighting the vulnerability of downstream settlements.
25 December 2025 – North Sikkim - Survey conducted in Lachung Valley, Yumthang Valley, and Yumesodong glacial lake region. Observations focused on snowmelt-fed lakes, river–lake interactions, and the Lachung River catchment characteristics.
Future Support and Local Coordination
During the field visit, it was observed that Mr. Roshan Thapa (Gangtok, East Sikkim) and Mr. Tshering O. Lachenpa (Lachen, North Sikkim) have agreed to support future field surveys. Their support will be crucial for local coordination, communication, logistics, and facilitation of permissions required for scientific surveys, including drone-based operations. Support is also expected from the SRM Sikkim Campus, with proper coordination through SRMIST Registrar’s approval and the forwarding of the official permission letter.
It was also observed that with a valid Delhi Army Office permit, along with Sikkim Government and Tourism Department permissions, movement through check posts is generally smooth, and no additional obstruction is expected. Vehicle permits are cleared at check posts when documentation is complete. North Sikkim access is available via Lachung, subject to weather and administrative clearance.
North Sikkim (Lachung–Yumthang–Yumesodong Sector): Field Observations
The area is characterized by a wide glacial valley with high-altitude alpine terrain.
Accessibility is primarily from Lachung, approximately 50 km from the main survey zones.
Drone operations may be conducted from Zero Point, Yumthang Valley, or Yumesodong Valley, depending on the month and snow conditions.
Surveys can be conducted only during forenoon hours (10:00 AM – 2:00 PM) as per permit conditions and climatic constraints.
In the upper reaches, the river remains frozen, while downstream sections are flowing; lake boundaries are difficult to identify clearly because of snow cover.
Significant human footprint and tourist movement were observed, making the ice-covered terrain rough and slippery, increasing operational risk.
East Sikkim (Gangtok–Zhuluk Route Sector): Field Observations
Snowfall had not started during the visit, and the area was accessible up to the lake sites. Similar accessibility may be expected in North Sikkim if routes remain open and weather conditions are favourable.
A temporary natural dam was observed, with high slope conditions immediately downstream, indicating potential geomorphic instability. A similar geomorphic setting was also noted in parts of North Sikkim.
Suggested Priority Sites for Drone-Based Surveys
Based on accessibility, terrain condition, and permit feasibility, the following locations are suggested as better candidates for future drone surveys:
East Sikkim: Huang Valley and associated lakes [Hangu lake, Tsomgo lake, Sarathang lake, Kupup lake]
North Sikkim: Yumthang–Lachung Valley [Sanglaphu Cho Lake and others]
These sites are comparatively easier to access, permit-friendly, and suitable for controlled drone operations.
Best Season for Field Visits and Drone Survey
Recommended Season: April to May and October to November
Winter (Dec–Feb): Limited access due to snow but useful for understanding frozen lake conditions
Monsoon (Jun–Sep): High landslide risk, not suitable for field or drone survey

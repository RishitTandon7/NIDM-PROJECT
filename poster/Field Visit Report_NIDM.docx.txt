North Sikkim (
Lachung
–
Yumthang
–
Yumesodong
 Sector): Field Observations
The area is characterized by a 
wide glacial valley
 with high-altitude alpine terrain.
Accessibility is primarily from 
Lachung
, approximately 
50 km
 from the main survey zones.
Drone operations
 may be conducted from 
Zero Point, 
Yumthang
 Valley, or 
Yumesodong
 Valley
, depending on the month and snow conditions.
Surveys can be conducted 
only during forenoon hours (10:00 AM – 2:00 PM)
 as per permit conditions and climatic constraints.
In the upper reaches, the 
river remains frozen
, while downstream sections are flowing; lake boundaries are
 difficult to identify clearly because of snow cover.
Significant 
human footprint and tourist movement
 were observed, making the ice-covered terrain 
rough and slippery
, increasing operational risk.
East Sikkim (
Gangtok
–
Zhuluk
 Route Sector): Field Observations
Snowfall had not started
 during the visit, and the area was accessible up to the lake sites. Similar accessibility may be expected in North Sikkim if routes remain open and weather conditions are favourable.
A temporary natural dam was observed, with high slope conditions immediately downstream, indicating potential geomorphic instability. A similar geomorphic setting was also noted in parts of North Sikkim.

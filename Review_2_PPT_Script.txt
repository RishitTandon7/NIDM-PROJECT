SLIDE 2: Problem Statement
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slide Text:
* Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs) present significant hydrological risks within the High Mountain Asia (HMA) region.
* The acceleration of glacial retreat, primarily driven by climatic variations, has resulted in the accumulation of substantial meltwater volumes behind unconsolidated terminal and lateral moraines.
* These natural barriers consist of loosely compacted glacial till and ice cores, lacking the long-term structural integrity necessary to accommodate increasing hydrostatic pressure.
* Rapid failure of these moraines can precipitate catastrophic downstream flooding, posing severe threats to adjacent settlements and critical infrastructure networks.
* Traditional methodologies for monitoring glacial hazard progression typically involve laborious manual digitization of satellite imagery or complex terrestrial surveying.
* Given the remoteness and structural instability of the targeted terrain, high-frequency physical monitoring is often unfeasible.
